What we are about

Three things we refuse to compromise on

01

Small scope, high finish

We would rather ship a tight three-hour game that people finish than a sprawling one they abandon. Scope is a design constraint, not a failure.

02

A visible hand

Distinct art direction over asset-store default. You should be able to recognise a WeDigi game from a single screenshot.

03

Built in the open

Devlogs, work-in-progress, honest post-mortems. Before we have a catalogue, the making is the thing worth sharing.
